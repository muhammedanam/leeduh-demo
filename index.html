— ATELIER DUBAI —

The Art of Slow Parfumerie

Founded in Dubai, LEE DUH was born from a desire to reclaim the quiet nobility of Arabian fragrance. In an era of mass-market luxury, our atelier operates on an intimate scale: crafting perfumes by hand, maturing ouds in cedar barrels for months, and pouring every flacon in limited vintages of five hundred numbered bottles.

01

Aged Botanical Ouds

Distilled from wild-harvested Cambodian and Assam aquilaria trees, then aged until the raw smoke yields to deep, velvety balsam and leather.

Wild-Harvested

Architectural Flacons

Each flacon is an homage to sculptural form: from diamond-faceted black obsidian to heavy crystal crown caps, designed to rest in the hand like a jewel.
