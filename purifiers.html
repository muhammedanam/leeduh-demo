Home & Car Purifiers

Dedicated smoke-free architectural reed diffusers and precision-milled automotive vent purifiers. Formulated with zero fine perfume crossover—engineered strictly for quiet architectural interiors and nocturnal grand tourer cabins.

Curated Formulations

The Complete Purifier Line

◆

Architectural Cold Diffusion

Natural dense reeds disperse Omani frankincense, dried fig suede, and white iris without heat, synthetic propellant, or flame. Calibrated for quiet modern salons, suites, and private dressing rooms for up to 16 weeks.

200 ml Vessel · 500 ml Decanter Refill
◎

Rotary Cabin Vent Clip

Precision-milled obsidian and gold anodized alloy clips slide cleanly into horizontal or vertical vehicle air louvers. Regulate cold alpine ozone, dark espresso, or green mandarin intensity by rotating the milled outer collar.

Anodized Diffuser · 2 Solid Scent Cores

Zero Perfume Overlap

Formulated entirely independently from our 4 Fine Perfume Extraits. Personal skin sillage and ambient room fragrance never clash, creating clean olfactory separation between yourself and your living or driving environment.
